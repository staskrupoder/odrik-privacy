Odrik — Privacy Policy

About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to Odrik for Android, developed by Stas Krupoder. Privacy contact: stas.krupoder@gmail.com. Effective date: 27 October 2026.

Information handled by the game

Odrik is an offline single-player game. The current Android game does not require an account and does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer. It does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or automatic developer-operated crash reporting. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or advertising identifier.

Local game data

The game stores your progress, inventory, character builds, game preferences, language, and control layout in its private application storage on your device. These files are used to save and restore gameplay and preferences. Debug or explicitly enabled diagnostic sessions may also write local technical logs, such as frame rate, engine version, graphics adapter, and application paths. The game does not automatically upload these files. Android automatic backup is disabled in the current build.

Sharing and external services

The game does not sell personal data or send local game data to advertising or analytics providers. Google Play and Android may independently process information relating to purchases, distribution, licensing, security, or platform diagnostics under their own terms and privacy policies. The game does not receive your payment card details. This policy describes the game and developer support; Google services are governed by Google’s privacy policy.

If you contact us

If you email the developer, your email address, message, and any files you choose to attach will be processed to answer your request and troubleshoot problems. Email is handled through the developer’s email service provider. Please do not send passwords, payment card information, or other unnecessary sensitive information. We do not automatically access your save files or logs; sending them is your choice.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you clear the app’s storage, uninstall the game, or otherwise delete the files. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes local progress and settings; copies you created separately must be deleted separately. The developer does not hold a server-side copy of your local saves. Support correspondence is retained only as necessary to address the request and meet applicable legal obligations. To request access to or deletion of correspondence you sent us, contact stas.krupoder@gmail.com. No in-game account exists to delete.

Security

Game saves and preferences use Android’s private application storage and operating-system access controls. No storage or communication method can be guaranteed completely secure. If you send support information by email, avoid including information that is not needed for your request.

Children

Odrik is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through the game. If you believe a child has sent personal information to the developer, contact us so that we can address the request.

Changes and contact

If the game’s data practices change, this policy will be updated and its effective date revised. For questions about this policy or your support correspondence, email stas.krupoder@gmail.com.
